How it works

Three views. One clearer workshop.

No sprawling system to learn. Just the information each person needs.

01

Update the job

Mechanics move a car through its repair stages with large, easy-to-tap controls.

02

See what needs attention

The office sees work by stage, parts delays, customer follow-ups and cars ready to collect.

03

Let customers check

A customer enters their job number and registration to see that job's stage and a plain-English update.

See the important stuff first

Know what's stuck before someone calls to ask.

The office dashboard separates active repairs from the jobs that need chasing. Waiting on a part for more than two days? That job is flagged red.

  • Jobs clearly grouped by repair stage
  • Parts-delay warnings and follow-up indicators
  • Ready-for-collection count
  • Changes saved in the same browser
