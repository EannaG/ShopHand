Behind the build

Kehoe’s Garage — Enniscorthy, Co. Wexford

Task 1

Hunch before AI

Original hunch — unchanged

Useful tool for small mechanics or garages across the company, not just limited to mechanical work. Could be useful for tyre shops or a similar and customisable product for small machine repair such as lawn mowers or even technology, phone and laptop repair shops.

Niche

Small independent general repair and servicing garages in Ireland 3-8 employees, including 1 individual managing the office.

Buyer: Garage owner or manager

Daily Users: Office administrator, service receptionist and the mechanics

Main problem: Constant update calls that interrupt repairs, delayed work or delayed parts leaves customer waiting and uninformed.

(INVENTED) “We are flat out working on cars, but the phone doesn’t stop ringing with people chasing updates, slowing us down further”
“If a part doesn’t arrive on time, the job gets pushed aside so we can work through the backlog and sometimes nobody remembers to update the customer.”

A user-friendly job tracker applicable for all sorts of workshops, with an integrated customer self-service status portal and an office facing dashboard. A digitalised display on waiting times or backlog.

How our niche handles it today: Paper job cards, whiteboards, spreadsheets, one phone, calls taken by receptionists or mechanics.

Other services: 19/10/2026

https://garagehive.ie/features/workshop-management-with-garage-hive/

https://motorworks.ie/

Business Model (assumption)

One-time setup €149

Monthly Subscription €39

PRD

Hypothesis: Small independent garages could show interest in paying €39 a month for a simple but user friendly job tracker tool that helps reduce customer status calls and forgotten parts delays leading to backlog build up.

Audience: Irish independent garages or general repair shops with 3-8 staff. Majority of staff are mechanics or workers leaving little office administrators to manage admin workload.

Call to action: Join our early-access waitlist.

Success measure: Number of interested garage owners who complete the waiting-list form in a future live test.

Out of scope: invoicing, payments, parts ordering and stock availability, automates SMS (for now) workshop scheduling (for now) and integration with existing CRMs or garage software.
